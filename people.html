PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

Stephanie M. Karst, PhD

Professor, Department of Molecular Genetics & Microbiology

Stephanie Karst leads a research program focused on how maternal and microbial signals shape health during early life. The laboratory studies bile acids as signaling molecules linking maternal physiology, the microbiome, neonatal immunity, growth, and developmental programming.

The lab combines virology, microbiology, metabolism, developmental biology, and mouse genetics to uncover mechanisms through which the early-life environment influences both immediate and long-term health.

MD-PhD Student
