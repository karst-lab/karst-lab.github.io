A collaborative group exploring how maternal and microbial signals shape early-life immunity, development, and lifelong health.

Principal Investigator

Stephanie M. Karst, PhD

Professor & Principal Investigator

Stephanie Karst is a Professor in the Department of Molecular Genetics & Microbiology at the University of Florida College of Medicine. Her laboratory investigates how maternal and microbial signals shape health during early life, with a particular focus on bile acids as signaling molecules linking maternal physiology, the microbiome, neonatal immunity, growth, and developmental programming.

The lab combines virology, microbiology, metabolism, developmental biology, and mouse genetics to uncover mechanisms through which the early-life environment influences both immediate and long-term health.
